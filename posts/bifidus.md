I went to Lidl after work, as I usually do. Spent about five minutes lurking in the cookie aisle.

There were these cookies. Some kind of spongy marshmallow on the inside, cookie on the bottom, orange marmalade, dark chocolate coating on top. Three or four layers. The box alone looked like it was designed by the Gucci porn department. There was a cherry version too.

I couldn’t look them in the eye.

I scanned the other cookies like there was actually some decision-making going on. There wasn’t. I knew what I wanted. The whole aisle knew. But it’s 90% women in Italian grocery stores — men don’t really shop here — and I could feel them watching. Not judging exactly. More like observing. An American man, alone, dilating in the cookie aisle. A nature documentary.

So I walked to the back corner of the store where they keep the yogurt.

I bought prune yogurt with bifidus. An act of undiluted self hatred that happened almost subconsciously. I didn’t even know what bifidus was.

Too ashamed to walk back down the cookie aisle on my way to checkout I slunk down the water and toilet paper aisle. Couldn’t risk one of them catching a glimpse of my loneliness, my nearly unchecked desire. I paid, I left, I came home with something old people eat when they can’t go to the bathroom.

I don’t know what I’m going to do tomorrow. But I know this. I will have to deal with those cookies. There will be a reckoning.

Hopefully the bifidus doesn’t hit before morning.
