Honey

I heard her park her aqua Panda like a backslash in a tiny spot on the street. She got out and slammed the door, its rusty cry her familiar siren call. I went to the window. She was fiddling in her purse. I watched from above as she stopped mid-motion and looked up at the sky like she just noticed it was there. Spring arrived this week and she's wearing this cream colored parka like it's still last week. Her eyes are closed and she seems to be reaching for the light. Suddenly she's back and the parka is off and she's moving fast as she stuffs it in her purse. I go to the door to greet her because if I'm not waiting with the door open she'll give me the hesitation when I do open it, the eyes narrowing thing, and it'll be ten minutes of eyes I can't decipher and she won't speak English because she knows I'm helpless without it. 

The doors always have to open for Honey.

She breezes through and walks around the room, assessing it I guess and drops her purse in the chair beside the couch and stands in front of me. She wants me to kiss her. She has red stain around her mouth.

"Melograno," she says. Rubbing the corners of her mouth with the tip of her finger, also red stained.
