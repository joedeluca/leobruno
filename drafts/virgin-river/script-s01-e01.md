```
VIRGIN RIVER
Season 1, Episode 1


EXT. MOUNTAIN HIGHWAY - DAY

Bird's eye view of a red car on a two-lane blacktop threading
through dense forest. Upbeat acoustic guitar -- The Weepies,
"The World Spins Madly On."

Camera pulls back. The car shrinks into a vast mountainous
landscape: a lake, low clouds, mist in the pines.

The image fades.


EXT. MOUNTAIN HIGHWAY - NIGHT

Same red car, close now. The road is narrower, the forest
thicker. Headlights cut the dark. The Weepies still playing.

INT. RED CAR - CONTINUOUS

MEL, mid-thirties, alone. Eyes on the road. Focused.

Her phone buzzes on the seat beside her.

                         MEL
               Oh great.

She glances down at the screen. In the rearview -- a semi,
closing fast.

The semi tries to pass. Mel is cut off. She loses control,
veers toward a stand of trees. The trees flash. The car spins.
We don't see impact.

The car has stopped. Mel raises her head, bracing over. She's
okay -- but she's hit her head. Blood on her forehead. She
sees it in the rearview, reaches for a tissue.

Her eyes in the mirror. Looking at herself. Remembering.

                                                    SMASH CUT TO:


INT. HOSPITAL - DAY (FLASHBACK)

A doctor leans in close, pen light in hand, checking her eyes.
This is DOCTOR MARK MONROE -- her husband. Alive.

                         DR. MONROE
               Well, that looks like a nasty bump.
               How do you feel?

                         MEL
               Not great.

                         DR. MONROE
               I'm not surprised. Word to the wise --
               that particular patient always requires
               restraints.

                         MEL
               Right. There wasn't a chart.

                         DR. MONROE
               It's your first day, huh?

                         MEL
               Yeah.

                         DR. MONROE
               Always get the chart. Ok?

                         MEL
               Yeah.

                         DR. MONROE
               Don't want to walk into a situation totally blind.
```
