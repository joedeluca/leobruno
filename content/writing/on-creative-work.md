# On Creative Work

There's something profound about making things. Not for profit, not for fame, but simply because the urge to create is irresistible.

## The Process

The best work happens when you stop trying to control every outcome. When you let the medium speak through you.

## Why It Matters

Because we're human. And humans need to make things to feel alive.
