---
title: "Ode to the Girl on the Bus"
author: "Leo Bruno"
date: "2026"
collection: "Writing from the number 30, Quartucciu to Cagliari"
---

I saw her board at Selargius
with her jeans like a flag
no country has claimed yet,
cinched at the waist
where the woman is
arguing with the child,
too long over her Air Force Ones
the brand they all wear
like bread wears its crust —
the ordinary uniform of becoming.

And oh, the others,
in black coats,
their hair pulled tight,
their identical armor
against the specific wound
of being anyone in particular.

But she —
she was reading them
the way the sea reads the cliff,
not with patience
but with eons of furious churn
that turns bastione to beach.

Her eyes moved
from face to face—
a tongue tasting the alphabet
for the beginning—the word
that would say _yes_.

I tell you
I have watched fruit ripen.
I have watched the octopus
solve the problem of the jar.
But nothing,
nothing requires more intelligence
than a girl of thirteen
standing in the aisle of a bus
trying to be seen
without being seen
trying.
