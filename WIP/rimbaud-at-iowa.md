FADE IN:

INT. IOWA WRITERS' WORKSHOP - SEMINAR ROOM - DAY

A circle of earnest MFA students. Coffee cups. Moleskines. One person has a therapy dog.

RIMBAUD (17, unwashed, drunk at 2pm, smells like absinthe and fuck-you) slouches in his chair. His manuscript pages are covered in wine stains and what might be blood.

WORKSHOP LEADER (50s, tweed skirt, kind smile, dead inside)
Arthur, welcome. We're so glad you're here. Now, I've read your poem "The Drunken Boat"—

RIMBAUD
Le Bateau Ivre.

WORKSHOP LEADER
Yes. And I think there's real... energy here. But I'm wondering if we might workshop some of the imagery? Make it more... accessible?

STUDENT 1 (cardigan, septum piercing, uses "praxis" unironically) I just want to say I really felt this piece. But I'm troubled by the colonial implications of the boat metaphor—

RIMBAUD
It's not a metaphor.

STUDENT 1
Right, but all language is—

RIMBAUD
IT'S NOT A FUCKING METAPHOR.

(pause)

The boat is drunk. I am the boat. The boat is me drunk on rivers. The rivers are drunk on me. There is no separation. There is only—

WORKSHOP LEADER
Arthur, I hear you. I do. But in workshop, we try to use "I statements" when giving feedback—

RIMBAUD stands. Buttons his coat (which is filthy). Picks up his wine-stained pages.

RIMBAUD
You people are already dead and don't know it.

He walks to the door. Stops. Turns.

RIMBAUD (CONT'D)
I'm going to Africa to run guns. When you're still here in twenty years, discussing whether to use "luminous" or "luminescent," remember: I chose the desert.

He exits.

STUDENT 2 (earnest, from Connecticut, working on a novel about his grandmother)
Should we... should we still workshop his sestina on Thursday?

WORKSHOP LEADER
(sipping coffee, already exhausted)
Yes, Marcus. We'll workshop Arthur's sestina on Thursday.

But Arthur never comes back.

Twenty years later, the workshop leader is still there, still kind, still dead inside.

Arthur is dead too. But he died in Marseille with one leg, having lived more in 37 years than all of them will live in 80.

FADE TO BLACK.
