Sample 001  /  Agency Website Copy

Bernstein-Rein:
Retailigent®

Complete website copy for Bernstein-Rein Advertising's brand relaunch — homepage hero, proprietary framework positioning, four core capability sections, and closing CTA. The strategic concept and initial brief originated with agency leadership. The copy on the page — every headline, section intro, and persuasive body paragraph — was redrafted by me during site development.

Client
Bernstein-Rein Advertising
Role
Copywriter / Front-End Developer
Type
Agency Brand Website
Scope
Full-site copy redraft
Note
Concept & first draft not mine

Homepage Hero  /  Primary Headline

Our ideas accelerate growth
where you need it most.

At Bernstein-Rein, we believe retail intelligence comes from consumer insights, a true brand purpose, and half a century of unrivaled experience. We put these beliefs together to accelerate growth. This approach is called Retailigent® — it's what drives our work every day.

Section 01
Mindset Modeling Consumer intelligence framework

A Consumer-Centric
Intelligence Approach

We use data science to go far beyond traditional market segmentation approaches. This helps us uncover the attitudes and behaviors that define your best customers. We can then launch the right message, at the right time, in the right place.

Craft note The original draft leaned on segmentation jargon. The redraft reframes the entire section around outcome — what the client gets — rather than methodology. "Right message, right time, right place" closes the loop in a single sentence.
Section 02
Metatasker Consumer behavior positioning

Consumers Continue to Change
the Price: Value Equation

Whether consumers are seeking price, convenience, quality, or personalized connections, value has never been more valuable. We believe firmly that this value mindset leads to unique consumer behaviors and connections.

Craft note The colon in "Price: Value Equation" is doing real work — it signals tension and trade-off, not just a category name. The final clause ("value has never been more valuable") is a compression that earns its slight paradox.
Section 03
InsideOut Retailing™ Agency differentiation

We Bleed, Dream
and Live Retail Every Day

No other agency in America launched, owns, and runs their own retail brand. We know how to build loyalty, increase average tickets, optimize footfall, convert clicks, and grow brands because we do it with our own money and name on the line every day.

We don't just know retail. We live it.

"We don't just know retail. We live it."
Craft note Separating the kicker into its own sentence is the whole move here. Everything before it is proof; the kicker is the verdict. The original draft buried both in the same paragraph.
Section 04
Heart of the Hustle™ Brand purpose framework

Declare Your Purpose and
Win Hearts and Minds

Like a heartbeat pulsating through the body, a brand's purpose flows through the entire brand experience. We uncover your customer's motivations, industry opportunities, and what makes you terrific — to define and leverage the heart of every hustle.

Craft note The biological metaphor earns its place because it connects physically to the section name. "What makes you terrific" is deliberately plain against the more figurative language around it — a small tonal surprise that grounds the section before the CTA.
Section 05
Closing Brand promise / CTA

Click to Brick.

We deliver customers from click to brick across every capability and channel. From regional upstarts to global brands, we leverage the same passion and profound experience for every client.

Your customer's journey is our journey.

Craft note "Click to brick" earns a headline of its own — it's the agency's whole value proposition in three words. The final line ("Your customer's journey is our journey") closes the site with an alignment statement, not a sell. That restraint is intentional.
